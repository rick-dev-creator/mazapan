# Obsidian theme

Obsidian in the theme, in every vault opened once (where
`.obsidian/appearance.json` is, up to three folders below your home): a
theme of its own, **Mazapan**, with the theme's colors, accent, fonts and
corners. Pick it once in Obsidian's Settings, Appearance, Themes; from then
on it follows every change of theme or accent. Which theme a vault uses
stays your choice: it's never overwritten.

Writes `.obsidian/themes/Mazapan/theme.css` and `manifest.json` in each
vault. Apps' Obsidian turns it on.
