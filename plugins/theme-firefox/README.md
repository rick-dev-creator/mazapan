# Firefox theme

Firefox and its forks (Zen, LibreWolf, Floorp, Waterfox) in the theme, in
every profile: the browser's frame (tabs, toolbar, address bar, menus,
sidebar, their corners too) and its own pages (new tab, settings,
add-ons). Websites keep their own colors. Thunderbird and Mullvad Browser
aren't reached.

Firefox reads it when it starts: restart it to see a new theme.

It's a stylesheet in each profile's `chrome` folder, imported from
userChrome.css and userContent.css, plus the one setting in user.js that
lets Firefox read them. Those files may be yours (Zen mods, your own CSS,
arkenfox): only Mazapán's lines are its own, the rest stays as you wrote
it. In high contrast mode the browser's colors are left alone.
