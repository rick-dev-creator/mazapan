# Window titles

`SUPER + T` shows a title bar on every window, and hides them again. New
windows get one too while they're shown. Also in the palette ("Show or
hide window titles").

Hyprland draws no title bars, but it does draw a bar with the title over
a group of windows. So showing titles makes each window a group of its
own, and hiding them undoes those groups; groups you made yourself are
left alone. So that nothing ends up in a group by accident, windows
aren't grouped automatically or by dragging one onto another.

The bars are in the theme: its colors, its interface font, its corners.

Settings: `key` (`SUPER + T`); `height` (20), the bar's height in
logical pixels, 12 to 48.
