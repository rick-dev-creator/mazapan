# Emoji picker

Every emoji (`SUPER + period`), by category, or found by its name: in
your language and in English, whichever you type. The ones you used last
come first. ↵ (or a click) types it where you were and copies it (`type`;
off, it only copies); the arrows move, Tab goes to the next category, Esc
leaves.

The emoji are Unicode's (emoji-test.txt, 18.0), without the skin-tone
variants; their names and keywords are CLDR's, in English, Spanish,
Portuguese, French and German.
Both are © Unicode, Inc., under the Unicode License v3
(https://www.unicode.org/license.txt). They're drawn with Noto Color Emoji.

What you used is in `~/.local/state/myarch-emoji/recent.json` (`recent`
of them, 32).
