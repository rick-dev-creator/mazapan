# GTK theme

GTK apps in the theme: GTK 4 and libadwaita ones (GNOME's), and GTK 3 ones
through adw-gtk3, which makes them look alike. Their colors, the windows'
corners, the fonts and their size, and dark or light, as the theme is.

It writes GTK's own files (`gtk.css` for GTK 4 and GTK 3, and GTK 3's
`settings.ini`) and sets the same choices in GNOME's settings (dark or
light, the GTK theme, the fonts), for the apps that look there.

GTK apps change when a theme is applied: while you go through themes in
the picker, they wait. Chromium, Chrome, Brave and Edge take their look
from here too (plugin Chromium theme).
