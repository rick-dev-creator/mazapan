# Screensaver

After a while without use (two minutes, or what you set), every screen shows
the time on the theme's background, its colors drifting slowly behind; the
time moves a little each minute so nothing stays burnt in. A key or the
mouse and it's gone — that key or click goes no further. A video, a call or
the bar's "keep awake" keep it away, as they keep the lock away.

It comes before the lock, which stays Idle's. With the theme's motion off,
nothing drifts. Optional: it's off until you turn it on (Settings › Plugins).
